# Decision Log

Record only decisions that affect architecture, scope, policy, data meaning, security, or acceptance.

| ID | Date | Decision | Reason | Alternatives considered | Consequences | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| D-001 | Initial | Version 1 is monitoring/decision support with synthetic telemetry; no physical control. | Safety and unavailable authorized OT inputs. | Direct control in MVP. | Control interfaces remain read-only/adapted. | Accepted |
| D-002 | Initial | Stage (m), discharge (m³/s), and volume (m³) are distinct domain quantities. | Required for correct hydrometry and reporting. | Generic `water_value`. | Schemas and UI must carry explicit type/unit. | Accepted |
| D-003 | 2026-08-23 | Use a TypeScript pnpm modular monolith with Fastify, React/Vite, and PostgreSQL/PostGIS. See ADR-0001. | Smallest deployment that supports typed contracts, GIS, deterministic domain modules, and future extraction. | Microservices/broker; Next.js full stack; mixed Python/TypeScript; SQLite. | Domain/contracts remain transport-neutral; no distributed infrastructure without evidence. | Accepted |
| D-004 | 2026-08-23 | Use append-oriented observation revisions, effective-dated governance records, and immutable report snapshots. See ADR-0002. | Corrections and historical reports must be explainable and reproducible. | Mutable current rows; reports rebuilt from latest data. | Reads distinguish current vs as-of state and derived values carry coverage/quality. | Accepted |
| D-005 | 2026-08-23 | Simulator telemetry enters through an adapter and streams to clients by SSE; the MVP has no physical-control command path. See ADR-0003. | Demonstrates live/replay workflows while preserving OT/IT safety and vendor neutrality. | Initial MQTT broker; WebSockets; vendor/PLC integration. | Future device/control work requires separate authorized architecture. | Accepted |
| D-006 | 2026-08-23 | Model the nominal/accounting water network as an authoritative directed acyclic graph of junction nodes and section edges, separate from administrative territory hierarchy. See ADR-0004. | Supports branches, merges, deterministic traversal, concurrent cycle prevention, and territory-safe cross-boundary continuity. | Waterway tree; duplicate edge table; geometry-derived topology; forced same-territory connectivity. | Graph direction is explicit; boundary reads redact foreign endpoint identity; calculations use a separately authorized canonical graph repository; physical recirculation needs separately governed future modeling. | Accepted |
| D-007 | 2026-08-23 | Make privileged mutations and their append-only audit event one transaction, and permit role administration only by a strictly higher-ranked, in-scope role. See ADR-0005. | A partial grant change or unaudited privilege change is unacceptable; conservative delegation prevents self/peer escalation before official IAM governance is available. | Best-effort audit after mutation; mutable audit rows; same-rank delegation; deletion of cancelled schedules. | Peer/system-admin provisioning requires a higher authority outside this API; scheduled grants are explicitly cancelled without deleting their original validity window; audit reads remain territory-authorized. | Accepted |
| D-008 | 2026-08-23 | Treat raw telemetry as unreliable until a governed validation revision, and separate `telemetry:correct` from operational ingestion authority. | Device claims must not become accounting truth before validation, and district/basin ingestion authority must not imply expert correction authority. | Treat adapter `valid` as immediately usable; reuse `telemetry:write` for corrections; mutate raw rows after validation. | Only valid automatic/expert/corrected revisions are usable by default; raw and estimated data remain visible but unreliable; hydrologists and senior in-scope administrators may create audited human revisions; P2-003 supplies validation rules/transitions. | Accepted |
| D-009 | 2026-08-24 | Govern automatic validation through immutable, distinct-author-approved, effective-dated sensor profile versions; defer rather than append when policy or trusted context is insufficient. See ADR-0006. | Automatic trust must be reproducible, scoped, auditable, and unable to invent universal hydrological policy. | Mutable/default profiles; trust raw predecessors; validate on receipt time; silently accept partial rule evaluation. | Only approved observation-time profiles can append automatic revisions; exact bounded bootstrap is explicit; prior rate/frozen context must already be governed-valid; missing interval policy remains unconfigured. | Accepted |

Add new rows and create an ADR in `docs/adr/` for decisions with meaningful technical trade-offs.
