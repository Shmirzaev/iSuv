A blocker should not stop independent implementation. Record it, choose a safe adapter/fixture/assumption where allowed, and continue.

Last reviewed after P7-006 on 2026-08-26: the live-table presentation change adds no external dependency. The software MVP is complete, while B-001–B-008 remain external rollout dependencies.
