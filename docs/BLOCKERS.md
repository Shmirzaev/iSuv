# Blockers and External Dependencies

A blocker should not stop independent implementation. Record it, choose a safe adapter/fixture/assumption where allowed, and continue.

Last reviewed after P7-001 on 2026-08-24: B-001–B-006 remain external rollout dependencies and no new blocker was introduced. Rating curves, coverage/cadence policies, derived volumes, allocation plans, official geometry/topology, measurement bindings, tolerance versions, travel times, accounting terms, alarm and incident policies, response targets, and every planned-versus-actual, balance, quality, availability, condition-signal, incident-metric, dashboard, live, map, alarm, incident, report, or audit result remain synthetic-only and explicitly ineligible for official use. P7 scenarios verify only rollback-isolated synthetic evidence and public decision-support behavior; they do not turn fixtures into official telemetry, policy, compliance, loss/theft findings, or infrastructure control. Authoritative authentication events still require a future real IdP integration. These dependencies do not block P7-002 security and authorization review.
