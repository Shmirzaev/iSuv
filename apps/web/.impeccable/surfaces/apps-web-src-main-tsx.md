## Scope and mode

Operate. Whole operator console (app shell plus workspaces), replacing the stock slate/sky-blue Inter look. Product truth, content, i18n, tests and behavior stay.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The record itself is the interface. Each station is a ruled ledger row with its own pen trace on millimetre paper. Refuses the KPI-card, rounded-panel, blue-accent dashboard template.

OWN-WORLD: Cool chart-paper white (#f1f5f3) ground with a pale teal-green millimetre grid (#8fbfb0) that is the real time axis, never wallpaper. Deep blue-black ink (#12253f) for traces and figures, a second blue (#3a5a8c) for planned/reference lines, correction red (#b3261e) only for struck originals, over/alarm and stamps. Double-ruled ledger columns, square corners, hairline rules, no shadows, no gradients. Approval and provenance shown as rubber-stamp boxes. Narrow Cyrillic-capable grotesque for labels, tabular typewriter-slab numerals for figures; no Inter. Status is text plus glyph plus hatch pattern, never colour alone.

STORY: An operator or auditor sees the readings as a record that can be trusted or doubted: gaps are bare hatched paper marked NO DATA, frozen sensors are flat ruled lines with a tag, corrections show the struck original with the revision inked above. They act on what is real.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Live Operations as a register. Left rail of station names and IDs with stage (m), discharge (m3/s) and volume (m3) in separate ruled columns with units in the column heads. Right two-thirds of each row is a 24-hour trace on millimetre paper running to a pen-edge "now" line at the right. Plan/reference line in secondary blue. Status column carries text, glyph and hatch. Synthetic-data stamp and data cutoff sit in the running header. Primary action is opening the station inspector by selecting a row.

FORM: Limnigraph strip chart and hydrological yearbook ledger, IMPECCABLE'S PICK, seed key a65db38d.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
